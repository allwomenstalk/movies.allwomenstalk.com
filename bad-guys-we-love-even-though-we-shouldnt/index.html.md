# 7 Bad Guys We Love in Movies and TV \(Even Though We Shouldn't\)

There are so many bad guys we love out there. Who hasn't sat through a movie or a TV show, thinking, "Wow, I probably shouldn't be enjoying this character so much"?! There are a million reasons to love a bad guy, whether it be looks, humor, or they're just so crazy you kind of just have to love them. Anyway, here are some of the bad guys we love even though we probably shouldn't.

## 1. Loki

![Loki](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31130544_7938_600x826.jpg)

It's kind of amazing that Loki is probably one of the most popular bad guys we love. If you spend any time on the internet then you will see that he is more loved than Thor, who is actually the hero of his movies. It doesn't seem to matter to anyone that Loki has tried to kill thousands of people because everyone just adores him so much. This likely has a lot to do with actor Tom Hiddleston and his brilliant portrayal across the [Marvel](https://www.marvel.com) Cinematic Universe, but nevertheless Loki is definitely loved.

## 2. Moriarty

![Moriarty](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31130628_6862_600x835.jpg)

Moriarty from the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk) series "Sherlock" is another bad guy that people just can't help but love. As the ultimate [Loki](#1)-level mastermind counterpart, even though he's crazy and a serial killer, people just love to watch him. The interactions between Moriarty and Sherlock Holmes definitely add to the love for the character. The intensity that the actor brings to Moriarty is amazing! You know it's wrong to love him, but you just can't stop.

| Character | Iconic Trait | Why Fans Love Them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loki | God of Mischief | Charismatic charm & complex motivations |
| Moriarty | Consulting Criminal | Unpredictable genius & sharp intellect |
| The Joker | Agent of Chaos | Captivating, intense screen presence |

## 3. Mr. Yang

![Mr. Yang](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31131031_4769_600x600.jpg)

Mr. Yang from "Psych" is a crazy serial killer, but the episodes with the character are so amazing that it's hard not to love Yang a little bit. There is a lot of mystery around who Mr. Yang is and once it was discovered, I think the audience loved Yang even more. The best part about this character isn't just the funny parts that come along with the insanity, but also the fact that the episodes are just so suspenseful and interesting to watch.

- Magnetizing screen presence and charismatic humor
- Complex backstories that make viewers empathize
- Unpredictable plot twists driven by villainous choices

## 4. Beetlejuice

![Beetlejuice](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31131054_5991_600x600.jpg)

I think some people forget that Beetlejuice is technically a bad guy. Many film lovers often ask: **is Beetlejuice evil**, or is he just a chaotic bio-exorcist? When discussing **bad guys in movies**, fans frequently wonder **is Beetlejuice a bad guy** or **is Beetlejuice a good guy** underneath all the gross antics. So **who is the villain in Beetlejuice**? While he's funny and doesn't seem to be too bad, in the end he really is the antagonist of the movie. Everyone who watches the movie loves the character, but that doesn't make him any less of a baddie. Whether we like it or not, when asking **why is Beetlejuice bad**, his mischievous motives confirm he is a true villain.

## 5. The Joker

![The Joker](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31145239_5162_600x884.jpg)

Here is another villain who may actually be more loved than the hero in his movies. The late Heath Ledger's representation of the Joker in the [DC Comics](https://www.dc.com) universe has been a fan favorite since the movie came out. While everyone loves Batman, it doesn't seem to compare to this complex character. Unlike [Beetlejuice](#4), who leans into supernatural slapstick, The Joker brings pure anarchy. Even though he's a real bad guy, people can't help but love him.

## 6. Dexter Morgan

![Dexter Morgan](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31145315_5919_600x800.jpg)

Dexter is one of those bad guys who just toes the line between good and evil. When viewers debate whether **is Dexter a bad guy**, it's clear he is a serial killer so he's technically a bad guy, but really he only kills other bad guys so that makes him good — right? If you enjoy brilliant masterminds like [Moriarty](#2), Dexter offers a fascinating psychological portrait. Everyone roots for Dexter even though it's probably wrong to hope a serial killer continues to get away with it, but who can help themselves? He's just a really great character.

## 7. Maleficent

![Maleficent](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31145428_1453_600x989.jpg)

Maleficent is one of the most loved Disney villains! She is one of the most popular, to the point that she got her own movie, and there was even a makeup and nail polish line at [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com) dedicated to her. What other bad guy can say that? Although she's evil and does try to control the kingdom by putting Princess Aurora into the deep sleep that earned her the title Sleeping Beauty, she does it so fabulously it's hard not to love her.

It's very conflicting when you start to love a bad guy; you know you're not supposed to and yet you can't help it! Which on this list do you love? Who would you add?
