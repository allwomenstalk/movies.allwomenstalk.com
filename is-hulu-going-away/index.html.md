# Will Disney Discontinue the Hulu Standalone App?

Disney has not announced a date to discontinue the Hulu standalone app. Starting in December 2026, Disney+ will offer a more complete Hulu experience, including live TV and select premium add-ons, according to [Disney’s November shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1744489/000174448926000065/fy2026_q4xerxex991.htm?pubDate=20261117). U.S. subscribers can still use the Hulu app.

The [standalone app shutdown question](#shutdown) matters most if you use Hulu for a live channel, a recording or an add-on. Check the [plan-by-plan guide](#by-plan) and [account steps](#what-to-do) before switching apps or changing what you pay for.

_Last updated November 23, 2026. This guide covers U.S. subscriptions._

## What Disney announced for December

Disney already puts Hulu programming inside Disney+. The December announcement concerns how much of the Hulu experience subscribers can get there, with live television and certain paid extras joining the programming already available. That matters if you currently use Disney+ for on-demand series and reach for the Hulu app when a game or local news comes on.

The shareholder letter describes the direction of the rollout. It gives no channel list, add-on list or device-by-device schedule. There is also no promise that an individual recording, profile or title will be available on the first day you see the change. For current access, Disney’s [guide to watching Hulu on Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/explore/articles/how-to-watch-hulu-on-disney-plus) is the more useful reference. For what is coming in December, the shareholder letter is the newer announcement.

Think of the two apps and the subscriptions as different things. The app is where you watch. The subscription is what gives you access to the programming, live channels or add-ons you pay for. Disney can make more of that programming playable inside one app while still selling Hulu as a subscription. When a headline says Hulu and Disney+ are merging, look for whether it is talking about the app, the catalog or the bill.

## What it means for your plan

Start with the plan name on your account page or latest receipt. Two households can both say they “have Hulu” and mean completely different things. One may pay for on-demand Hulu directly, while another gets Hulu with Disney+ through a bundle or pays for live TV.

What to check

Hulu on its own
Disney says eligible Hulu subscribers can use the same MyDisney login to watch Hulu through Disney+ without changing plans or paying extra. Check eligibility and try a specific show before relying on the Disney+ app.

Disney+ on its own
Disney+ offers a selection of Hulu programming. If a particular series is missing, check the title and your access before assuming your Disney+ subscription includes the whole Hulu library.

A Disney+ and Hulu bundle
Sign in with the MyDisney account connected to the bundle, look for the Hulu section and test the shows you watch most. Keep the Hulu app handy for programming you can still find only there.

Hulu + Live TV
Check the channel or game you care about on the device you use to watch it. Disney named live TV for December, but the announcement does not list every channel, location or feature.

A premium add-on or Live TV Only plan
Check your exact entitlement. Disney’s current [U.S. help guidance](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-is-hulu-going-away) lists limitations for Live TV Only and Hulu add-ons. The December letter says “select” add-ons and supplies no list.

A subscription through another company
Check the bill from the company that sold you the subscription. The plan name, pricing and activation steps can differ from buying directly from Hulu or Disney+.

A separate Disney+ subscription is not automatically necessary just because you open the Disney+ app. Disney explicitly describes access for eligible Hulu-only subscribers using their existing MyDisney login. If the app takes you to a checkout page, stop and confirm that you are signed into the right account before paying. A different email address can make an existing subscriber look like a new customer.

The Hulu section of Disney+ also is not a complete inventory of what you personally can see. Access depends on your plan, and Disney’s current consumer guide says certain titles are still available only in the Hulu app. If an episode appears on your phone in Hulu and fails to show up on the living-room TV in Disney+, search for that episode by title and compare the profile, plan and app before deciding it has left streaming. ## When will Disney discontinue the Hulu standalone app?

There is no announced shutdown date for the Hulu standalone app. December 2026 is when Disney says the expanded experience will begin on Disney+. Keep Hulu installed while you check whether your live channels, recordings and paid add-ons are available through Disney+.

There is also a difference between Disney saying that live TV will be available through Disney+ and Disney detailing how it will work. If you routinely record an evening show and watch it a day later, seeing a live channel inside Disney+ only answers part of your question. Check whether you can find and play your recording first. The same goes for a favorite channel whose availability depends on your home location.

Viewers outside the United States may already see a Hulu hub in Disney+ where they previously saw Star. That is a different change from U.S. households moving between two apps. Use your local Disney+ help site and plan details if you subscribe outside the United States.

## Will Hulu or Disney+ cost more because of the merge?

Disney’s letter gives no price change tied to the December rollout. If your bill changed earlier in the fall, compare the line items and billing dates on your statements before assuming the app update caused it. A subscription rate change, an expiring promotion and an added premium channel can all make this month’s total look different.

The best comparison is the plan you pay for today versus the plan you would actually need to keep the same programming. Check [Disney+ plans and prices](https://help.disneyplus.com/article/disneyplus-price) and [Hulu’s current offers](https://www.hulu.com/welcome), then compare those with the renewal amount in your account. For live channels, use the [Hulu + Live TV plan page](https://www.hulu.com/live-tv) and check your ZIP code. Pay attention to whether a displayed figure is a temporary sign-up offer and whether a bundle price includes the ad tier or live TV you want.

On November 23, Disney’s U.S. offer page showed regular monthly renewals of $12.99 for the Disney+, Hulu Bundle and $21.99 for Bundle Premium after its advertised six-month offers. Hulu’s own live-TV page listed $99.99 per month for Hulu (With Ads) + Live TV with Disney+ and ESPN. Those are prices on the existing plans and offers. Check the current page and your own renewal before buying.

If two adults in a household have been paying for Hulu and Disney+ separately, it can be worth looking at the bundle rate. First check which email owns each subscription and whether either account already gets access through another company. Take a minute to compare the next renewal amounts. That is easier than unwinding a second subscription bought from the wrong profile.

## What should you do right now?

- **Find the billing account.** Check your current subscription and note who bills it. Look at the email on your latest receipt if more than one person manages streaming in your household.

- **Use the Disney+ app on the screen where you actually watch.** Sign in with the eligible MyDisney login you already use. A successful test on your phone is helpful, but it leaves open what your TV, console or streaming stick can do.

- **Try the shows or features you regularly use.** Play an on-demand episode. If you pay for live TV, check a channel you regularly watch. If you pay for an add-on, try a title from that add-on as well. Keep Hulu installed while you verify each one.

- **Read the profile-linking screens carefully.** If Disney+ offers to link a Hulu profile, check that it is your profile before proceeding. Once linked, spot-check your watchlist and where playback resumes on a show. Keep using Hulu for anything you cannot yet find on the other app.

- **Revisit once the features show up in December.** Check Disney support for the rollout details and any account notices. You can decide which app to open by default when you know what your subscription can play there.

If part of your household mostly watches on-demand and someone else depends on a live channel, those two viewers can test the apps separately. There is no need to make everyone switch screens on the same evening. For now, keep the access you already pay for working and treat Disney+ as another place to try it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Disney discontinuing the Hulu standalone app in December 2026?

Disney has announced a December start for expanded Hulu access inside Disney+. As of November 23, it has given no date for shutting down the standalone Hulu app.

### Can I watch Hulu on Disney+ with a Hulu-only subscription?

Eligible U.S. Hulu-only subscribers can sign into Disney+ using the MyDisney login they use for Hulu. Disney says there is no extra charge or plan change for eligible access. Current exceptions and availability are listed in its U.S. help guidance.

### Will I get Hulu + Live TV on Disney+?

Disney named live TV as part of its December rollout. The announcement does not specify every plan configuration, channel, add-on or device. Keep using Hulu for live features you have yet to verify inside Disney+.

### Will I lose my watchlist or recordings?

Disney offers Hulu profile linking for eligible accounts. The December announcement gives no complete account-by-account guarantee for watchlists or DVR recordings. Check the items you care about in both apps before removing Hulu from your devices.

### Do I have to buy a new bundle?

The December announcement gives no universal switch or purchase requirement. Look up the plan you already have, test your existing login and review what the app actually lets you play before spending money on a new plan.
