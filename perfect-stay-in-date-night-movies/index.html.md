# 18 Best Date Night Movies for a Cozy Stay\-In Evening

Date night used to be quite the outing—getting all glammed up for dinner, dancing, or a night out on the town. Nowadays, the best date night often consists of comfortable sweats, a cozy couch, and your favorite streaming platform. The only remaining question is: what should you watch? Whether you are looking for lighthearted laughs, thrilling suspense, or romantic drama, here is a curated list of date night movies perfect for your next cozy stay-in evening.

Key elements for a great stay-in movie night:

- Comfortable seating and cozy blankets

- Delicious snacks ready before pressing play

- A movie both of you can get invested in

| Movie Title | Genre | Perfect Vibe |
| --- | --- | --- |

| The Big Sick | Romantic Comedy | Heartwarming & Real |
| Baby Driver | Action / Music | High Energy & Thrilling |

## 1. Set It up

A classic rom-com for new age couples about two New York assistants who conspire to set up their demanding bosses in order to get more time in their personal lives. Wittily written and charming, it is a fun choice when searching for an easygoing date night film. Explore more [stay-in date night movies](https://movies.allwomenstalk.com/) on our site, or check out details on [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4012202/).

## 2. Icarus

A documentary about the Russian doping scandal during the Winter Olympics. Even if you don’t follow the Olympics, this is an intriguing story that will shock you about steroid use in the Olympics. It unfolds like a political thriller that will keep both of you hooked from start to finish.

## 3. The Phantom Thread

A movie about a couture dress designer and the relationship he has with his muse. Set in 1950s London, Daniel Day-Lewis delivers a masterclass performance in a film that blends romance, drama, and unexpected tension.

## 4. Thor: Ragnorak

An action movie for him and Chris Hemsworth for her, this superhero movie entails the god of thunder trying to save his people from Ragnorak. Packed with colorful visuals and comedy, it keeps the energy high throughout date night.

## 5. Murder on the Orient Express

A murder mystery movie based on Agatha Christie’s novel, with an all-star cast that will have you and your partner guessing “Who’d done it?” Set aboard a luxurious train, it keeps you guessing right up to the final reveal.

## 6. The Babysitter

A 12-year-old boy named Cole is left at home with a hot babysitter who just so happens to me in a demonic cult. And on the itinerary . . . sacrificing virgins. And it looks like Cole might be next. Cole must outsmart a gang of cult members to survive the night in this wild comedy horror flick.

## 7. Logan Lucky

From the director of the Oceans serious, comes a heist movie about siblings who believe they are cursed with bad luck and decide to change their luck by robbing a NASCAR race track. It is full of humor, clever twists, and a stellar cast.

## 8. The Little Hours

A raunchy comedy about nuns who are greeted by a handsome, young visitor that has these ladies howling and ready to strike, leaving this convent anything but quiet. His presence sends the convent into hilarious chaos, making for an irreverent night of comedy.

## 9. Moonrise Kingdom

A Wes Andersen film about two twelve-year-old runaways in love, with their entire town and family trying to locate them before a big storm hits their small island. Anderson's signature aesthetic shines bright in this story of young love.

## 10. Baby Driver

An Edgar Wright film, with a great soundtrack, Baby Driver is about a teenage boy who is a professional getaway driver who listens to music as he drives, to drown out the hum in his ears. However, Baby wants out after he meets the girl of his dreams and makes this last score the drive of his life. Read more on the official [IMDb page](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3890160/).

## 11. The Big Short

The story of the 2008 financial crisis and “the financiers who saw the crash coming and made money off of it.” Smart editing, celebrity cameos, and sharp dialogue make complex financial topics entertaining. Check the [IMDb overview](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1596363/) for cast details.

## 12. Chef

A movie about finding one’s purpose after becoming lost, chef Carl Casper finds himself unmoored after quitting his life in L.A. and moves back home to Miami, where he, along with his ex-wife, friend and son, start a food truck together. A feel-good movie about rediscovering passion and family bonding.

## 13. Drinking Buddies

Best friends and coworkers, Kate and Luke are drinking buddies. And these buddies decide to go on vacation together with their significant others, even though its clear that there’s something between them. Add tight corners and mixed feelings, and you have a recipe for complicated.

## 14. The Big Sick

A romantic comedy that starts like any other. Boy meets girl, boy falls for girl, then boy and girl begin dating. However, problems arise when a boy, Kumail, is pressured by his Pakistani parents to marry a Pakistani girl. Girl, Emily, falls ill and is placed in a coma and Kumail finds himself bonding with Emily’s parents while struggling to get along with his own.

## 15. What We do in the Shadows

A mockumentary about ancient vampires trying to keep up with an ever-changing world while cohabitating in a flat together. The only difference between us and them is that we’re what they need to survive. It is witty, endlessly quotable, and a great pick for comedy lovers.

## 16. Southside with You

A fictional retelling of the first date between former President Barrack Obama and First Lady Michelle Obama. The film captures their philosophical conversations, mutual respect, and romantic sparks over an afternoon in Chicago.

## 17. The Shining

A psychological thriller about a family that moves into a Colorado hotel in the middle of winter, that raises more questions than it answers. Note to self: this movie is not meant to be viewed alone—definitely a movie best experienced snuggled together on the couch!

## 18. Amelie

A French film about a waitress, who through anonymous acts of kindness, brightens the lives of others. And through these acts of kindness, Amelie discovers a world of possibilities. Though the film is in French, it is bound to still move you. Subtitled but deeply enchanting, it remains a beloved cinematic masterpiece.

- Keep a shared watch-list to save decision time
