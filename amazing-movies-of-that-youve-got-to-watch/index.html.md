# 7 Best Movies to Watch from 2013 That You Need to See

With so many amazing movies of 2013 to choose from, narrowing down the absolute best films to watch from 2013 was no easy task, especially with such a rich variety of genres hitting theaters that year! As a huge film enthusiast who loves everything from romantic comedies to edge-of-your-seat thrillers, I've put together a curated watchlist. Whether you are revisiting modern classics or looking for good movies to watch 2013 delivered, here are seven must see movies 2013 offered that still hold up amazingly today!

| Movie Title | Primary Genre | Ideal Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Hunger Games: Catching Fire | Dystopian / Action | Fans of intense book adaptations |
| Fast & Furious 6 | Action / Heist | High-octane thrill seekers |
| Monsters University | Animation / Family | All-ages family movie night |

## 1. The Hunger Games: Catching Fire

The first on our list of 2013 movies to watch is [The Hunger Games: Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Catching_Fire). Surprisingly, I did not start the Hunger Games series until this second installment came out in 2013, but I was truly captivated from start to finish! This blockbuster remains a landmark in dystopian cinema. The costume design, arena concept, and makeup are truly one of a kind. Not to mention, Jennifer Lawrence delivers an incredible performance that solidified her as a powerhouse role model and actress.

Key elements that make this one of the top 2013 movies to watch:

- Jennifer Lawrence's emotional and fierce performance as Katniss Everdeen
- Stunning visual effects and arena environment
- A gripping storyline that expands the scope of Panem

## 2. Fast & Furious 6

[Fast & Furious 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_%26_Furious_6) is another absolute must-see from 2013! What is better than a blockbuster packed with fast cars, fantastic soundtrack beats, and non-stop action? Beyond being a thrilling entry in the franchise, watching this film now is a wonderful way to reminisce on Paul Walker's talent and charismatic screen presence (may he rest in peace). While Vin Diesel initially announced the 7th entry for 2015, the franchise's legacy continues to be celebrated by action movie fans worldwide.

## 3. Now You See Me

[Now You See Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Now_You_See_Me_(film)) is another incredible thriller movie of 2013! The premise is clever, fast-paced, and endlessly fun to watch. In the storyline, four illusionists known as The Four Horsemen take magic to the next level by pulling off bank heists during their performances and staying steps ahead of the law. If you enjoy slick heist thrillers, this is definitely one of the good movies to watch 2013 brought to the big screen. (If you love high-stakes storytelling like [The Hunger Games: Catching Fire](#1), this clever mystery is right up your alley!)

## 4. This is the End

[This Is the End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Is_the_End) is a fabulous comedy movie of 2013 that delivers nonstop laughs. What makes it so funny is that it features exaggerated versions of nearly every top comedian and celebrity playing themselves during an apocalyptic party. The concept is so delightfully silly that you can't help but laugh out loud! While the humor can be crude at times, it remains one of the most entertaining films to watch 2013 gave us.

## 5. After Earth

The father-son chemistry between Will Smith and Jaden Smith brings a unique dynamic to screen. After Earth is a sci-fi, action-packed movie with an intriguing futuristic setup. It brings intense moments and emotional beats as a father and son navigate a hostile post-apocalyptic Earth. It's a noteworthy addition to the list of movies for 2013 for sci-fi enthusiasts.

## 6. Snitch

Snitch is another crime thriller exploring a powerful father-son relationship. Starring Dwayne Johnson, the story follows a desperate father whose teenage son is framed in a drug conspiracy. Driven by love, the father agrees to go undercover for the DEA to reduce his son's sentence. This gripping drama really makes you reflect on how far a parent would go to protect family. (Much like the high-octane family loyalty seen in [Fast & Furious 6](#2), this film delivers emotional intensity.)

## 7. Monsters University

Monsters University is a great family movie and an inspiring story for viewers of all ages. Serving as a prequel to Pixar's Monsters, Inc., it's fascinating to look back at how Mike and Sulley first met in college. The animation quality and heartwarming message make it a standout choice for family movie nights. (If you're building a watchlist of lighthearted comedy alongside films like [This Is the End](#4), this animated favorite is perfect for all ages.)

I hope this watchlist inspires your next movie night with these amazing movies of 2013! There is nothing better than grabbing some popcorn and settling in with a great film. What are some of your own favorite 2013 movies to watch? Share your top picks in the comments below!
