# 10 Best Angelina Jolie Movies of All Time

**Angelina Jolie movies** are universally popular for good reason. Beyond her iconic screen presence, her versatility as an actress, director, and producer has solidified her place as one of Hollywood's most captivating figures. While early critics sometimes overlooked her dramatic depth, exploring **Angelina Jolie's most famous movies** reveals an incredible range—from raw psychological dramas to blockbuster action hits. Whether you are looking for her Academy Award-winning performances or high-octane favorites, here is a breakdown of the **best Angelina Jolie movies** that truly define her career. Take a look at some of her finest work and see if your top pick made the list!

| Movie Title | Release Year | Primary Genre |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Lara Croft: Tomb Raider | 2001 | Action / Adventure |

## 1. Girl, Interrupted

Without question, _Girl, Interrupted_ stands out as one of **Angelina Jolie's most famous roles** and a personal favorite. Her portrayal of Lisa Rowe—a charismatic yet dangerously volatile psychiatric patient—was captivating, earning her an Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress. Long before taking on blockbuster roles like [Lara Croft: Tomb Raider](#3), Jolie demonstrated undeniable dramatic power here, perfectly complementing Winona Ryder's central performance. You can read more about the film's accolades on its [IMDb profile for Girl, Interrupted](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0184516/). While there were noticeable differences between the film adaptation and Susanna Kaysen's original memoir, Jolie's electric energy remains the heartbeat of the movie.

## 2. Gia

My close friend Adam would never forgive me if I omitted this biographical film from the list! It remains a standout **Angelina Jolie movie** and one he introduced me to years ago. Contrary to popular belief, many fashion historians consider Gia Carangi to be the world's first true supermodel, preceding figures like Janice Dickinson. Sadly, Carangi's groundbreaking career was tragically brief and marked by severe personal turmoil. Jolie delivered a heartbreaking performance as the vulnerable, troubled model on HBO's [Gia on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0123865/). Her portrayal captured both raw vulnerability and magnetic screen charisma during a pivotal moment in her own early career.

## 3. Lara Croft: Tomb Raider

Angelina brought unmatched charisma and athleticism to this 2001 adaptation of the popular _Tomb Raider_ video game franchise. Playing the legendary archaeologist Lara Croft, she proved she could single-handedly lead a major studio action franchise. While her dramatic performance in [Girl, Interrupted](#1) showcased her emotional depth, this role established her as an international action icon capable of performing complex stunt sequences with confidence.

## 4. The Bone Collector

This crime thriller ranks high among **Angelina Jolie's most popular movies**, largely because it represented a fresh direction in her filmography at the time. Prior to playing rookie officer Amelia Donaghy, Jolie was often cast in rebellious or anti-hero roles. Here, she held her own alongside Denzel Washington's brilliant quadriplegic forensic expert, bringing grit and determination to a suspenseful investigation.

## 5. Changeling

Directed by Clint Eastwood, _Changeling_ remains one of the most deeply affecting **Angelina Jolie films** ever made. Set in 1920s Los Angeles, her Oscar-nominated performance as Christine Collins—a mother searching for her missing son while fighting systemic police corruption—is devastatingly powerful. Details on this historical drama can be found on the official [Changeling page on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0824747/). Jolie's understated emotional restraint kept the period piece grounded and profoundly moving.

Key elements that make this film stand out include:

- A gripping historical narrative based on true events from 1928 Los Angeles.
- Exceptional period-authentic costume and set design.
- An Oscar-nominated Lead Actress performance anchored by quiet resilience.

## 6. Wanted

To be completely honest, _Wanted_ is one of my personal guilty pleasures among **Angelina Jolie action films**. While film critics may debate its placement among her prestige dramas, the stylized direction by Timur Bekmambetov makes it an incredibly fun watch. Jolie plays Fox, a skilled assassin in a secret fraternity, culminating in an unforgettable final sequence. Fans of her work in [Mr. and Mrs. Smith](#7) will thoroughly enjoy the high-octane energy she brings to every scene here.

## 7. Mr. and Mrs. Smith

When discussing top-tier action-comedies, few titles match the cultural impact of _Mr. & Mrs. Smith_. Playing an assassin married to another covert operative (Brad Pitt), Jolie delivered sharp comedic timing alongside explosive action sequences. Check out details on [Mr. & Mrs. Smith on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0356910/). Despite massive media attention during its 2005 release, the film's undeniable chemistry and entertaining premise ensured its lasting popularity.

## 8. Original Sin

This 2001 romantic thriller showcases both Jolie's dramatic intensity and striking screen presence. Co-starring Antonio Banderas, the film takes place in 19th-century Cuba and centers on a web of deception, identity, and passion. Much like her role in [Wanted](#6), Jolie imbues her character with mystery, keeping viewers guessing until the very end.

## 9. Alexander

Oliver Stone's historical epic _Alexander_ received mixed reviews upon release, but Jolie's fierce portrayal of Queen Olympias remains a highlight. As the scheming mother of Alexander the Great (Colin Farrell), she stops at nothing to ensure her son's rise to power. Jolie excels at playing formidable, complex women who refuse to be sidelined, making her scenes commanding and memorable.

## 10. A Mighty Heart

No comprehensive guide to **Angelina Jolie movies** would be complete without _A Mighty Heart_. Based on Mariane Pearl's memoir detailing the search for her abducted journalist husband Daniel Pearl, the film requires immense emotional sensitivity. Jolie's quiet, dignified performance avoids melodrama, delivering a powerful tribute to resilience under tragic circumstances.

If you haven't explored these **best Angelina Jolie movies**, they offer an incredible window into her evolution as an actress. Beyond the tabloid headlines and red carpet appearances, her commitment to diverse roles across drama, action, and history speaks for itself.
