# 7 Best 80s Chick Flicks You Need to Watch \(Iconic Classic Movies\)

While modern cinema gives us plenty of romantic comedies, the 1980s delivered a golden era of high-energy dance routines, swoon-worthy romances, and unforgettable friendships. Whether you are looking for timeless romance, big laughs, or comforting nostalgia, the best chick flicks from this decade still hold up remarkably well. If you are putting together a list of must-watch 80s chick flicks, these seven iconic films are absolute essentials for your next movie night.

## 1. “Footloose”

Long before the 2011 remake, the 1984 original [Footloose on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0087277/) introduced audiences to small-town dance rebellion. Kevin Bacon stars as Ren McCormack, a city teen who moves to a small town where rock music and dancing have been banned. Teaming up with Ariel, played by Lori Singer, Ren challenges the local council to bring life and music back to the community.

Comparing classic originals with modern remakes is always a fun exercise for movie lovers. The vibrant soundtrack and classic 80s fashion give this original cut a unique vintage charm that the newer version just cannot duplicate. If you enjoy dance-heavy romances like [Dirty Dancing](#point3), this one belongs at the top of your watchlist.

Key highlights of this 80s favorite:

- Iconic title soundtrack by Kenny Loggins
- Kevin Bacon's famous warehouse dance sequence
- Memorable supporting performances by Sarah Jessica Parker and Chris Penn

## 2. “Top Gun”

Premiering in 1986, [Top Gun](https://www.paramountplus.com/movies/top-gun/) proved that action-packed blockbusters can double as classic chick flick movies that couples enjoy watching together. The film captivated global audiences by blending high-stakes naval aviation with genuine emotional depth.

At its heart, the story focuses on the bond between Maverick (Tom Cruise) and his trusted radar intercept officer, Goose (Anthony Edwards). Alongside the high-flying adrenaline, the film delivers a steamy romance between Maverick and Charlie (Kelly McGillis), an astrophysicist and elite Top Gun instructor. With a chart-topping soundtrack and unforgettable volleyball scenes, it remains one of the top chick flick movies of the decade.

## 3. “Dirty Dancing”

If you are craving an 80s chick flick heavy on romance and unforgettable music, Dirty Dancing is the ultimate pick. Released in 1987 but set in the summer of 1963, the story follows Frances "Baby" Houseman (Jennifer Grey) during a family vacation at a Catskills resort.

Baby quickly becomes fascinated by the resort's charismatic dance instructor, Johnny Castle, played brilliantly by Patrick Swayze. As Johnny teaches Baby the rhythm and moves of dirty dancing, sparks fly and a deep romance blossoms. From the lift practice in the water to the famous finale dance, this film defines what makes 80s romantic cinema so enduring. For another tear-jerking story about deep personal bonds, check out the southern drama in [Steel Magnolias](#point7).

## 4. “Mannequin”

This 1987 fantasy romantic comedy offers a delightfully unique premise that could only succeed in the eighties. Andrew McCarthy stars as Jonathan Switcher, an artistically minded window dresser working at a struggling department store. Jonathan falls in love with a mannequin he crafted, named Emmy (Kim Cattrall), who comes to life whenever they are alone.

Together, Jonathan and Emmy create stunning store displays that boost sales dramatically and revitalize the business. When rival stores attempt to sabotage their work and put Emmy in danger, Jonathan has to save her and protect his chance at true love. It is a lighthearted, eccentric romance featuring the Oscar-nominated hit song "Nothing's Gonna Stop Us Now."

## 5. “Three Men and a Baby”

For viewers looking for genuine laughs and heartwarming moments, Three Men and a Baby delivers on every front. Directed by Leonard Nimoy, this hilarious film became the biggest box office hit of 1987.

The comedy explores what happens when three wealthy New York bachelors—played by Tom Selleck, Ted Danson, and Steve Guttenberg—unexpectedly find a baby girl left on their doorstep. As they struggle to navigate diapers, feeding schedules, and unexpected chaos, the trio discovers the joys and challenges of fatherhood. Watching these three leading men bond with baby Mary makes this one of the most endearing comedy films of the era.

What makes this comedy a fan favorite:

- Charming chemistry between Selleck, Danson, and Guttenberg
- Relatable and funny parenting mishaps
- A perfect blend of slapstick humor and genuine warmth

## 6. “Look Who’s Talking”

Hitting theaters in 1989, [Look Who's Talking on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0097778/) stands out as one of the most charming romantic comedies of the late 80s. The film introduces Mollie (Kirstie Alley), a single mother whose labor begins while riding in a taxi driven by James (John Travolta).

James helps Mollie get to the hospital and quickly becomes a regular presence in her life. What makes the movie exceptionally funny is the inner monologue of baby Mikey, voiced with quick wit by Bruce Willis. As James and Mollie form an unconventional romance, Mikey offers hilarious observations on adult behavior from a baby's perspective.

## 7. “Steel Magnolias”

Widely regarded as the queen of iconic chick flick films, Steel Magnolias (1989) captures the unbreakable bond among a close-knit group of southern women in Louisiana. The story centers around Truvy's beauty parlor, where the women gather to exchange gossip, laughter, and support through life's triumphs and tragedies.

Featuring a legendary cast that includes Julia Roberts, Sally Field, Dolly Parton, Shirley MacLaine, Olympia Dukakis, and Daryl Hannah, the film expertly balances humor with heartbreak. Its tender portrayal of mother-daughter love, resilience, and female friendship earned critical acclaim and multiple award nominations, solidifying its status as an enduring classic.
